Experimental Learning Prototype

Learn AI by experimenting with it.

PromptLab is a gamified learning environment designed to help students develop critical generative AI literacy, prompt-engineering skills and the ability to evaluate AI-generated information.

Research Perspective

PromptLab explores whether students can develop stronger critical AI-literacy skills through experimentation, controlled comparison and reflection.

Guiding question:

Can students develop stronger critical AI-literacy skills by experimenting with AI outputs and reflecting on their behaviour rather than learning prompt-engineering rules through explanation alone?

Step 1

AI Literacy Pre-Assessment

Before starting the activities, estimate your current confidence and understanding.

1. How confident are you when evaluating whether an AI-generated claim is reliable?

2. How familiar are you with prompt engineering?

Activity 1

Prompt Builder

Build a stronger prompt by controlling four important variables.

Why this matters

A prompt is not simply a question. Context, task, audience and constraints communicate what the user actually wants the AI system to do.

Activity 2

AI Output Detective

AI-generated text can sound convincing even when some claims are unsupported or incorrect.

Fictional AI-generated response

University Scenario

Which statement best describes how you should respond?

Key principle

Generate ≠ Verify.

Fluency, confidence and detail are not guarantees of factual accuracy.

Activity 3

Prompt Battle

Compare two prompts and predict which one is likely to produce a more useful response.

Prompt A

Explain university life.

Prompt B

You are an advisor helping an international master's student understand university life in Germany. Explain the main academic, social and administrative aspects in simple language. Give three practical examples and organise the answer using headings and bullet points.

Activity 4

Reflect on Your Learning

Reflection turns an interaction with an AI system into a learning experience.

What did you learn?

Write a short reflection. You can mention something that surprised you, a prompting technique you learned, or something you would verify differently in the future.
